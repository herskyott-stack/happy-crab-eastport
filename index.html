Waterfront seafood, a full bar & a great deck.

In its own words: “Cozy Restaurant, Popular Bar. Juke Box… Great Deck…” Happy Crab is a waterfront seafood restaurant and bar on Water Street in Eastport — come for the chowder and the lobster roll, stay for the music.
